Refund & returns policy

Furniture is a big decision. That's why you get 60 days to live with it first.

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Questions? hello@hartwood.demo

On this page 60-day trial How returns work Refunds Damaged items Exceptions

1. The 60-day home trial

Every piece comes with a 60-day home trial, starting the day it's delivered. Sit on it, spill coffee near it, let the kids test it. If it's not right for your home, we'll collect it and refund you — collection is free in our standard delivery zones, and there are no restocking fees.

To qualify for a full refund, the piece should be:

  • In good, lived-in-but-loved condition — normal use is expected, damage from movers or pets may reduce the refund.
  • Complete — all cushions, legs, hardware and shelves included.
  • Returned with your order number (that's all the proof of purchase we need).

2. How to start a return

  • Step 1 — Email returns@hartwood.demo with your order number and what you'd like to return.
  • Step 2 — We'll reply within 1 business day to schedule a free pickup window — the same two-person crew model as delivery. You don't box anything; we wrap it.
  • Step 3 — We collect the piece, and your refund is processed when it reaches our workshop.

Prefer an exchange? Same process — tell us the piece you'd like instead and we'll deliver it at the same appointment where possible.

3. How refunds work

  • Returns are inspected within 2 business days of arriving at our workshop.
  • Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Delivery fees are refunded in full for damaged, defective or incorrect items. Otherwise, the standard delivery fee ($79, if paid) is non-refundable — the crew still made the trip.
  • Orders placed with a promo code are refunded at the discounted price you actually paid.

4. Damaged, defective or incorrect deliveries

Inspect your piece at delivery if you can — our crew will wait. If you find a problem later:

  • Email hello@hartwood.demo within 14 days of delivery with photos of the damage.
  • Choose a repair, replacement or full refund — including all delivery fees. Structural issues discovered any time within the 10-year frame warranty are repaired or replaced free.

5. Exceptions & final sale

  • Clearance and "final sale" pieces (marked at checkout) can't be returned for change of mind, but are always covered if damaged or defective.
  • Gift cards are non-refundable.
  • Custom-stained or modified pieces can be returned for 50% store credit during the trial, unless damaged or defective.
  • Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights.

6. Late or missing refunds

If 10 business days have passed since our confirmation, check with your bank first — posting times vary. Then email hello@hartwood.demo and we'll trace it the same day.

7. Outside our delivery zones

Returns from freight-only addresses follow the same 60-day window, but return freight is the customer's responsibility unless the piece arrived damaged or incorrect. See the shipping policy for zone details.
