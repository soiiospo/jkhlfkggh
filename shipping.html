Shipping & delivery policy

Furniture doesn't fit in a mailbox. Here's how it gets to your living room instead.

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Questions? hello@hartwood.demo

On this page Delivery service Rates & times Delivery day Zones & freight Issues

1. What's included — every order

All furniture orders ship with our standard two-person room-of-choice delivery:

  • Carried to the room you want — up to two flights of stairs included.
  • Full assembly of anything that needs it, and leveling on uneven floors.
  • All packaging removed and recycled by us.

Small items (ottomans, stools, nightstands bought alone) may ship by parcel carrier with doorstep delivery — the checkout always shows which service applies.

2. Rates & delivery times

  • $79 flat rate per order, or free on orders over $999 (after discounts).
  • In-stock pieces: delivered within 5–10 business days, on a date you choose at scheduling.
  • Made-to-order pieces (custom stain, modified dimensions): 4–6 weeks, with the estimate shown on the product page.

3. Delivery day

  • After checkout, our team calls or emails within 2 business days to schedule your delivery window (4-hour windows, Tuesday–Saturday).
  • You'll get a text when the crew is ~30 minutes away.
  • Someone 18 or older must be home to accept and inspect the delivery.
  • Please measure doorways, halls and stairwells beforehand — our product pages list full dimensions, and a piece that can't physically fit can be refused at no cost to you.

4. Delivery zones & freight orders

  • Standard zones: the contiguous United States.
  • Alaska, Hawaii and remote addresses ship by third-party freight with curbside delivery; fees are quoted at checkout and the free-delivery threshold doesn't apply.
  • We don't currently deliver internationally, to PO boxes, or to APO/FPO addresses — furniture and mailboxes really don't mix.

5. Order tracking

You'll receive an email when your order enters routing, another when your window is booked, and day-of texts from the crew. Parcel-shipped small items include a carrier tracking link.

6. Delivery issues

  • Damage: note it with the crew before they leave if you can, and email photos within 14 days — repair, replacement or refund per our refund policy.
  • Missed window: if nobody 18+ is home, one redelivery is free; further attempts are $49 each.
  • Rescheduling: free up to 48 hours before your window, by email or phone.
  • Address changes: free any time before your order enters routing.

7. How we pack

Furniture travels in reusable padded blankets on our own trucks wherever possible — no mountains of cardboard. Parcel items ship in recycled, recyclable packaging only.
