Our story

Built by cabinetmakers, priced for real homes.

Hartwood & Co. began in a Portland cabinet shop in 1974. Fifty years on, we still cut every frame from solid, certified timber — because furniture should be repaired and refinished, never landfilled.

  • Solid wood frames — no particleboard, ever
  • Washable, replaceable covers on upholstered pieces
  • Free spare parts for the life of the piece
