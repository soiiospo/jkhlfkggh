Privacy policy

We make furniture for your private spaces. We treat your data with the same respect.

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Applies to hartwood.demo and all Hartwood & Co. services

On this page What we collect How we use it Cookies Sharing Your rights Contact

1. Information we collect

Furniture delivery is personal — someone has to know where your home is. We collect only what that takes:

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email and phone number, so our delivery crew can reach you on the day.
  • Delivery details — street address, access notes (stairs, elevator, gate codes) you choose to share at checkout.
  • Order information — what you bought, assembly preferences and your order history.
  • Newsletter signup — your email address, if you subscribe. One click unsubscribes you forever.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & browsing data — browser type, pages viewed and rough region, used in aggregate to improve the shop.
  • Cart contents — stored locally in your own browser (localStorage); in this demo storefront it never leaves your device.

We never see or store your full card number — payments run end-to-end through our payment processor.

2. How we use your information

  • To schedule, deliver and assemble your furniture — including calling you about your delivery window.
  • To handle the 60-day home trial, returns, refunds and warranty claims (see our refund policy).
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To send the newsletter, only if you subscribed.
  • To detect fraud and keep the store secure.
  • To learn, in aggregate, which pieces people love — so we can build more of those.

3. Cookies & local storage

  • Essential — your cart between pages and visits (your browser's localStorage).
  • Preferences — things like dismissed banners and region.
  • Analytics (aggregate only) — anonymous page-view counts. No cross-site tracking, no ad profiles, no data brokers.

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time; the shop still works, but your cart won't persist between visits.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell personal information. Data goes only to the partners required to run the shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Delivery & assembly crews — name, address, phone and your access notes, for your scheduled window only.
  • Payment processor — to charge your card securely (card details go directly to them, never to us).
  • Email service — your address, solely to send emails you asked for.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

5. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — ask what data we hold and fix anything wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and history (we retain what's legally required, e.g. tax records).
  • Opt out — unsubscribe from marketing in one click; order and delivery emails will still be sent.
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a common, machine-readable format.

Email privacy@hartwood.demo to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days.

6. Data retention & security

Order and warranty records are kept for up to 10 years (our frames are warranted that long — we need to know what we built you). Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with team access limited to those who need it to help you.

7. Children's privacy

Our shop is for adults furnishing their homes. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided any, contact us and we'll delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date, and significant changes will be emailed to subscribed customers before taking effect.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? privacy@hartwood.demo or Hartwood & Co., 800 Millwright Ave, Portland, OR 97209.

This is a demo storefront; this policy is illustrative and not legal advice.
